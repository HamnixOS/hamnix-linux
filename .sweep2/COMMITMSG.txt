sweep2: the coverage the first sweep ran out of time for, with the counts read from the logs

Cut from 9e893e58 (tip of port/tier1-syscalls at the time). Control for every
red is b87174c344bbc542374323d7d996affff480c511, exported with git archive into
.sweep2/control and bootstrapped there, so no tree was disturbed to take it.

93 distinct gates attempted, 85 produced a PASS/FAIL verdict. The eight that
did not are not gates and are named in the report: private_ns.sh is a sourced
library, cpuprobe.sh is a helper that wants a pid, vk_icd_survey.sh prints
UNDETERMINED by design, and drag_one, drag_why, lat_null_control,
panel_reaction and wsys_op_census emit measurements with no assertion. Every
gate ran with stdin redirected from /dev/null and the chain echoes one line per
gate whether it passes, fails or times out; the counts above come from that
file rather than from any runner's own summary.

ONE NEW RED, and it is a count rather than a gate.

  gates_are_private.sh   tip 2 passed / 15 failed
                         control 2 passed / 12 failed

The three gates in the difference are wsys_enum_policy.sh,
wsys_srv_identity.sh and wsys_srv_readlat.sh -- all added since the control,
all starting the window system on the host without priv_ns_reexec. Not fixed
here: other agents hold those files, and the gate prints the two-line remedy
itself.

THE REST OF THE REDS REPRODUCE AT THE CONTROL OR ARE MINE.

  pixcmp.sh        under 8 load threads: tip 2 FAIL / 5, control 2 FAIL / 5.
                   Pre-existing race between the device composite and the
                   readback. Solo it passes at both, which is how it nearly
                   got reported as a regression.
  de_probe.sh      identical single failure at both ends ("the typed keys
                   reach the focused window"). Pre-existing.
  distro_menu.sh   a missing prerequisite, not a defect: build/image/
                   alpine.ext4 does not exist anywhere on this host. With
                   distro.ext4 staged in it went 8 PASS / 11 FAIL to
                   14 PASS / 5 FAIL, and every survivor is an Alpine
                   assertion. The gate is byte-identical to the control.
  two_namespaces.sh  same cause, stated by the gate: "no alpine image".
  de_appmenu_installed.sh, de_appmenu_brisk.sh
                   both were MY harness. A long TMPDIR pushed a QEMU monitor
                   socket past the 108-byte sun_path limit; unsetting TMPDIR
                   fixed the first, and the second needed it unset entirely
                   because priv_ns_reexec gives the gate a private /tmp that
                   the host path is not inside. Both pass now: 11/0 and 17/0.
  wsys_srv_transport.sh  the known budget arm, plus a second arm that cannot
                   be isolated on a shared host: it asserts no abstract
                   socket named hamnix-wsys/*/srv is bound, and
                   /proc/net/unix shows other agents' compositors live in
                   that namespace. SRV_WORK does not reach it.

TWO THINGS AGAINST MYSELF. The first classifier keyed on "qemu" and missed
that 39 gates reach a VM through scripts/hamlinux_image.sh, so a four-way pool
briefly ran concurrent image builds; it was killed inside three minutes and
nothing from it is counted. The same gap let scanout_desktop.sh and
scanout_try.sh run, and they take DRM master on the owner's card --
scanout_desktop fired its 45s watchdog to give the console back. Those two and
cap_power_ab.sh are now on the do-not-run list beside the ten display-blocked
gates.

Perf-sensitive gates ran serially behind a loadavg < 2.0 gate, logged per gate;
they landed at 1.45-1.99, which is at the threshold rather than under it, so
the absolute numbers in those logs deserve less weight than their verdicts.
